# Team Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We will treat each other with respect and kindness, welcome different viewpoints, give and accept feedback gracefully, and own our mistakes.

## Decisions

We decide by consensus. If an idea is rejected, we talk through why, and we may turn it into a new decision for everyone to agree on.

## Attendance

- Everyone shows up regularly.
- Anyone who misses a meeting is held accountable.
- Missed meetings are covered by filling the person in through the group chat, including their assignments.

## Assignments

- All assignments are finished on time.
- No one divides the work up at the last minute.
- **First missed or poorly done assignment:** a warning.
- **If it keeps happening:** we communicate with the professor.

## Participation

- We communicate through the group chat.
- We share files through Google Drive.

## Meeting Times and Format

| Day | What happens |
|-----|--------------|
| Monday | Create the new assignment in the project class |
| Tuesday / Thursday | Stand-ups to check on progress |
| Next Monday | Assign new tasks if the current ones are complete |

## Agenda and Notes

Quick, brief notes are added to the Google Drive.

## Promptness

Communicate early and have a good reason. We adjust the schedule when needed.

## Conversational Courtesies

- No phones during meetings.
- No tangents.

## Enforcement and Feedback

We communicate directly with the team. Warnings come first, and the professor is brought in only if the problem continues.
